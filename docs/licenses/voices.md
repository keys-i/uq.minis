# Animal recordings

Recordings are bundled locally and only requested by the browser after a click with animal voices enabled
Attribution and source links are kept in this credits file, available through the repository link in the application footer
These are recordings of living species; extinct animals have visual, imagined calls and no fabricated audio

All excerpts were converted to mono MP3 at 24 kHz and 48 kbit/s, volume-balanced towards −24 LUFS and given short fades
Pitch and playback speed are unchanged
